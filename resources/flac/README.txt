FLAC 1.5.0, 64-bit Windows build, from the official Xiph.Org release:
https://github.com/xiph/flac/releases/tag/1.5.0 (flac-1.5.0-win.zip, Win64 folder; SHA-256
53f1500f0d6e7c61379d7fee50d4a9f7f504c650009506d9ba015530d76c0dde, as published in the release notes).

MusicDesk runs flac.exe to cut single-file albums into tracks (SPEC section 22). flac.exe needs libFLAC.dll
beside it, and nothing else.
